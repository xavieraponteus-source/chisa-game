Latest Development Update

What's new in Version 1.6

The latest update refreshes the way your adventure begins while keeping the same magical flight experience.

01 / WELCOME

Cleaner Get Ready screen

The original Get Ready artwork is back at full size. A more compact welcome panel displays the essentials without an extra scroll.

02 / CONTROLS

Clear Sakura-style buttons

The artwork stays, and each top control now has a name underneath: Power, Support, Ranks, Sound, and Pause.

03 / ABILITIES

Real power previews

Six illustrated ability previews use their matching in-game pictures, with short descriptions to help players get started.

04 / COMPETE

Online rankings

Choose to share scores online, compare with friends by Chisa code, or enjoy the game offline with a personal best.

The Magic of Flight

Know your powers

Every picture below matches the ability artwork used in the game. Pick up a charm during your flight to activate its power.

Spirit Shield

Protects from one pillar hit for a limited time.

Time Vortex

Slows the world so you have more time to react.

Double Points

Earn twice the points, including Perfect bonuses.

Echo Darkness

Reveal dark surroundings with pulses of red light.

Mirror World

Flight reverses. Swipe left to dash while mirrored.

Kitsune Awakening

A rare fox transformation with fire and a huge score boost.

Quick Guide

Easy to learn. Hard to master.

Tap to fly

Time each tap to move through the gates.

Swipe to dash

Make a deliberate long forward swipe. Dash charges automatically.

Get close, then rewind

Earn rewind charge from close calls. Use it after a mistake.
