CHISA: Moonlight Flight

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This policy explains how CHISA: Moonlight Flight handles information in its game and online leaderboard. The developer’s privacy contact is CHISA privacy and support requests.

1. Information handled by the game

  • On your device: the game stores a randomly generated player identifier, your chosen nickname, and your personal best score. These support saved preferences and score tracking.
  • Online leaderboard: when an online flight starts or a score is submitted, the game sends your player identifier, nickname, flight/run identifier, and score to the CHISA score service. Flight records can also include start and completion times, and score updates during play in versions supporting checkpoints.
  • Technical information: online requests necessarily expose an IP address and connection information to the hosting services. Those services may process technical logs for delivery, security, reliability, and abuse prevention.
  • Privacy inquiries: if you contact the developer, your contact/account information and the information you provide are used to handle your request.

The game does not require an account login, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone for gameplay. Avoid using your real name or other personal information as your leaderboard nickname.

2. Public leaderboard information

Your nickname and scores are visible to other people on the public leaderboard. Depending on the leaderboard view, flight dates and completion status may also be visible. Your randomly generated player identifier is used internally to associate your records; it is not intended as a public display name.

3. How information is used

Information is used to run the game, remember your preferences and best score, maintain online rankings and flight history, identify score submissions, protect the service against abuse, troubleshoot failures, and respond to privacy requests.

4. Advertising and tracking

The current game includes no advertising SDK or third-party analytics SDK. It does not use an advertising identifier or track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising. Leaderboard information is not sold.

5. Hosting and service providers

The website and this policy are hosted on GitHub Pages. Online leaderboard requests are handled by the CHISA score service hosted through ChatGPT Sites infrastructure. Hosting providers process the information needed to deliver and secure those services.

Relevant provider information: GitHub privacy statement, OpenAI privacy policy, and Cloudflare privacy policy. Information may be processed in countries other than your own.

If you obtain the game through Apple’s App Store or TestFlight, Apple separately handles information associated with its distribution, purchase, and testing services under Apple’s privacy policy.

6. Retention and deletion

Local preferences and scores remain on your device until the app’s stored data is removed, or the browser’s website data is cleared. Removing local data does not by itself remove online leaderboard records.

Leaderboard records are retained to maintain rankings and flight history. To request access, correction, or deletion of your online records, open a request at CHISA privacy and support requests titled “CHISA privacy request.” GitHub issues are public and require a GitHub account. Include only your public game nickname and approximate flight dates or scores. Do not post your email address, real name, passwords, identity documents, or other sensitive information. The developer may ask for additional information to identify the correct records and avoid deleting another player’s data.

Technical logs and backups may be retained according to hosting-provider practices and applicable requirements. Clearing a device or changing a nickname should not be treated as confirmation that server records have been deleted.

7. Children and families

Players should not enter personal information into nicknames. A parent or guardian can contact the developer to request review or removal of a child’s leaderboard information. The game does not request a player’s age or birth date.

8. Security

Leaderboard requests use HTTPS. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information, but no system can guarantee absolute security. Public nicknames and scores should be treated as information shared with other players.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the game’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will identify the latest revision. For privacy questions or requests, contact CHISA privacy and support requests.
